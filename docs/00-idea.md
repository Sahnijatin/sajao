# The idea

A person wants to redecorate a room, or has just moved into a new flat and wants a makeover. Today she either pays a designer, or scrolls shopping apps and guesses whether things will fit and look right together.

## What she does in Sajao

1. Takes a photo or video of the room. One space at a time; the whole home comes later.
2. Fills in a short brief:
   - what to include: paint, lights and ambience, furniture (sofa, chairs, tables, shelves), decor, rug, wall art, and more
   - budget
   - the vibe she wants, with a reference image if she has one
   - an optional free-text field for anything else
3. In the background the app maps the exact geometry and dimensions of the space from the capture.
4. She gets three design variations for that exact room, shown in a live view with every item placed in real time.
5. She can drag, rotate and fix anything the way she wants.
6. She can also skip shopping entirely and only rearrange what she already owns.
7. Tapping any item takes her to buy it through our affiliate link, which earns a commission.

## What the product must get right

- **Accuracy everywhere.** It must work in any room and setting, not be precise in one room and wrong in the next.
- **No dependence on phone hardware.** India is heavy on Android, much of it older. A tape measurement is requested when needed.
- **Low AI cost.** Use AI, including a chatbot, while keeping LLM spend minimal.
- **Self-improving.** The system should retrain automatically and become more precise over time.
- **Premium feel.** Modern, elegant, fun and easy to use on both Android and iOS, with motion and effects that create a "wow" moment. The visual language follows the LiveMap design system.
- **Lean business.** Low expenses, maximum value from each user, and room for revenue beyond affiliate commission.
