# The idea

A person wants to redecorate a room, or has just moved into a new flat and wants it to feel like home. Today she either pays a designer, or scrolls shopping apps and Instagram and guesses whether things will fit and look right together.

## What she does in Sajao

1. Picks the room: living room or bedroom.
2. Walks slowly around the room while the screen guides her. The scan drafts her floor plan.
3. Measures length, width and ceiling height with a tape when the app highlights each one. The room is now verified.
4. Fills a short brief: budget, style, what to include, who lives there, Vastu preference.
5. Sees three designs in 3D, then her own photo redesigned, with lamps glowing.
6. Drags, rotates, swaps and locks anything; flips between day and night.
7. Taps any item to buy it, or buys the whole room as a list.
8. Shares an automatic before and after reel of her room.
9. Or skips shopping and only rearranges what she already owns.

## What the product must get right

- **Accuracy everywhere.** Every room, every phone. No fit claim the system cannot back.
- **No dependence on phone hardware.** Heavy work runs on our servers; the app stays light.
- **Products people actually want.** Curated items from the brands that make spaces beautiful.
- **Everything that makes a room.** Lighting, paint and wall finishes, flooring and rugs, curtains, furniture, art, decor and plants.
- **Vastu, as close as possible.** Honest about its limits, careful about direction.
- **Premium, Apple-like feel.** Minimal, modern, sleek, with motion, haptics and real wow moments, following the LiveMap design system.
- **A reveal worth sharing.** Sajao makes the before and after reel for her.
- **Never stops suddenly.** Costs are measured and capped, with fallbacks instead of failures.
