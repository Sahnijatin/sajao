# The idea

## The user

Someone in an Indian city who wants a room to feel finished and beautiful but does not know where to start. They save reels and Pinterest boards, scroll Flipkart and Amazon for hours, and still cannot picture how pieces will look together or whether a sofa will fit. Most are on Android, many on mid-range or older phones. Ages range from Gen Z renters to families in their forties who own their homes.

## The problem

- **Imagination gap.** People cannot picture their own room transformed.
- **Fit anxiety.** They do not know what will physically fit, so they buy nothing or return things.
- **Scattered shopping.** Good products are spread across many retailers and brands with no designer tying them together.

## The promise

Sajao is transformation-first. You should feel that Sajao understands your home, not that you are operating a design tool.

| Promise | You give | You get back |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Understand my room | Photos or a short video; optional tape values | A Room Profile: shape, size with an error band, light, doors, windows, what you already own |
| Understand my taste | A few swipes, budget, who lives there, optional references | A Taste Profile in plain words you can correct |
| Show my possibilities | One tap | Three designed transformations of your own room |
| Help me execute | Taps | Shopping list with prices, buy or rent links, a dimensioned plan, Vastu guidance and a share reel |

## What it must get right

Three things, all at once and never as separate features: the redesign must excite (visual quality), it must make physical sense (spatial credibility), and every item must be real and buyable at the shown price (commercial utility).
