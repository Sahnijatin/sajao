# The idea

A person wants to redecorate a room, or has just moved into a new flat and wants it to feel like home. Today she either pays a designer, or scrolls shopping apps and Instagram and guesses whether things will fit and look right together.

## What she does in Sajao

1. Picks the room: living room or bedroom.
2. Sweeps her phone around the room for about 30 seconds while the screen guides her.
3. Measures one highlighted wall with a tape. The room is now verified.
4. Fills a short brief: budget, style, what to include, who lives there, Vastu preference.
5. Watches her floor plan draw itself and her walls rise while the designs are made.
6. Sees three designs, first as her own photo redesigned, with lamps glowing.
7. Drags, rotates, swaps and locks anything in a live 3D view; flips between day and night.
8. Taps any item to buy it through our affiliate link, or buys the whole room as a list.
9. Shares an automatic before and after reel of her room.
10. Or skips shopping and only rearranges what she already owns.

## What the product must get right

- **Accuracy everywhere.** Every room, every phone. No fit claim the system cannot back.
- **No dependence on phone hardware.** India is heavy on Android, much of it old. Heavy work runs on our servers; the app stays light.
- **Products people actually want.** Curated items from the brands that make spaces beautiful, not whatever a feed returns.
- **Everything that makes a room.** Lighting, paint and wall finishes, flooring and rugs, curtains, furniture, art, decor and plants.
- **Vastu, as close as possible.** Honest about its limits, far more careful about direction than compass apps.
- **Premium, Apple-like feel.** Minimal, modern, sleek, with motion, haptics and real wow moments on both platforms, following the LiveMap design system.
- **A reveal worth sharing.** The empty-to-finished reveal is what people post. Sajao makes that reel for her.
- **Lean cost.** AI is used where it improves the result, with a measured cost per room and hard caps.
