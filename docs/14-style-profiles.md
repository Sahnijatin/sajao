## What the references teach

1. **The empty-to-finished reveal spreads.** Two cut before to after from the same camera angle; Sajao's share reel does this from scan camera positions.
2. **Lighting is the hero.** Lighting is required in every design, lamps are shown on, day and night is core.
3. **Walls matter as much as furniture.** Wall finishes are first-class materials; gallery walls get frame-arrangement templates.
4. **Plants, rugs and art finish a room.** Included by default.
5. **Some looks are carpentry.** Built-ins become a lead later; v1 uses freestanding products.
6. **Indian "before" rooms look alike.** The golden set must look like them.

The reference library grows over time; permissions are logged per creator.
