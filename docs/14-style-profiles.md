## What the references teach

1. **The empty-to-finished reveal is the format that spreads.** Two of them cut before to after from the exact same camera angle. Sajao's share reel does this automatically from scan poses.
2. **Lighting is the hero.** Warm, layered light makes every "after". Lighting is required in every design, lamps are shown on, and day and night is a core feature.
3. **Walls matter as much as furniture.** Texture, colour blocks, niches and gallery walls recur. Wall finishes are first-class materials; gallery walls get frame-arrangement templates.
4. **Plants, rugs and art finish a room.** They are included by default.
5. **Some looks are carpentry.** Built-in TV units, niches and panels are custom work; v1 uses freestanding products, with a "get this made" lead later.
6. **Indian "before" rooms look alike.** Vitrified tile, white walls, wall ACs, grills. The golden set must look like this.

The reference library grows over time; each new reference is tagged to a profile or starts a new one.
