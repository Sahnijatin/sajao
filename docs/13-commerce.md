# Commerce: affiliate links and other revenue

Marketplace affiliate commission alone is thin and its tracking windows are short. Plan for direct brand deals and a second revenue line from the start.

What the published rates say (they conflict between sources, so confirm on official rate cards):

| Program | Home and furniture rate | Tracking window | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Amazon India via Cuelinks | 1.52% for furniture, home, kitchen | Not stated | [Cuelinks 2026-27 guide](https://www.cuelinks.com/blog/amazon-affiliate-commission-rates-guide/) |
| Amazon India direct | 4 to 5% quoted for Home and Kitchen | 24 hours | [Fluxnote guide](https://fluxnote.io/guides/amazon-india-affiliate-commission-rates-2026) |
| Flipkart | 5 to 8% home furnishings, with a per-order cap around 3,000 to 5,000 rupees | 7 days | [Fluxnote guide](https://fluxnote.io/guides/flipkart-affiliate-commission-rates-2026) |

On a 40,000 rupee room, that range pays roughly 600 to 3,200 rupees, and only if she buys inside the window. Furniture is a slow decision, so many purchases will land outside a 24-hour window.

How to fix the economics:

- **Direct brand partnerships.** Negotiate higher rates and longer attribution, tracked by coupon code or server-to-server postback instead of cookies.
- **Bring her back through a fresh link.** Saved designs, price-drop alerts and back-in-stock alerts each restart the tracking window.
- **Whole-room list.** One screen grouping items by retailer, with one tap per retailer.
- **Paid extras.** Photoreal renders, extra rooms, and a human designer review for a fixed fee.
- **Service leads.** Paint needs a painter; shelves need a carpenter. Referral fees from home-service providers.
- **Rental option.** Offer furniture rental partners for renters.
- **Sponsored placement**, clearly labelled and never allowed to break fit, budget or style filters.
- **B2B later.** White-label the engine for furniture brands and builders; sell anonymised demand insight (what sizes and styles people want and cannot find).

Link plumbing to build: every buy tap goes through our own redirect service, which logs user, design, product and position, then forwards to the retailer deep link with a sub-ID. Network reports are reconciled against these logs daily to give revenue per design. That number is also a training signal for the ranker.

Two rules to respect: disclose affiliate links in the app, and check each program's terms on in-app use and on displaying prices before launch.
