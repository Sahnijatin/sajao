# Principles and scope

These decisions hold everything else together. A change that breaks one of them needs a new entry in the decision log first.

1. **Geometry is measured, never generated.** The room comes from reconstruction plus a tape check. No language or image model ever decides a dimension or a position.
2. **AI decides taste and appearance; code decides fit.** An AI designer chooses the program, pairings and products. A solver places them. An independent verifier re-checks every hard rule. A failed design is never shown.
3. **Every item is real and curated.** Each product is in stock, has verified dimensions, matches a style profile, and has a working, app-safe affiliate route before it is listed.
4. **The reveal is the product.** Her photo, redesigned, with true positions and sizes, then a shareable before and after reel.
5. **Light app, heavy server.** The phone captures and displays. Reconstruction, segmentation, rendering and design run in the cloud.
6. **Check everything AI produces.** Every model output passes a measurable check: geometry rules, product-likeness, schema validation or human-calibrated scores.
7. **Honest by design.** Error bands are shown, renders are labelled illustrative, and Vastu is presented as guidance, as close as possible.
8. **Learning comes in v2.** v1 uses pretrained models, hand-authored rules and small taste models trained before launch. Automated retraining from usage waits until real users exist.

## v1 scope

- India, consumer app, Android and iOS, any phone with a camera.
- Living room and bedroom.
- Categories: lighting, paint and wall finishes, flooring and rugs, curtains, seating, beds, tables, storage, wall art, decor, plants.
- Room-level and whole-home Vastu guidance.
- Fully free at launch. No paywall appears anywhere in the app.
