# Competitive landscape

Nobody combines her own measured room, real products from many retailers, an automatic full-room design and an editable 3D view. Each competitor has two or three of those four, and none is built for India.

| Player | What it does well | Where it stops |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [IKEA Kreativ](https://www.markhub24.com/post/ikea-india-s-digital-room-planning-experience-bridging-online-commerce-and-physical-retail) | Scans the room, erases furniture, places products at true scale. The closest product to this idea. | IKEA products only. No automatic full-room design to a brief. Little India-specific work. |
| [Wayfair Decorify, Paintit.ai](https://www.mogul.com/post/shoppable-interior-design-apps) | AI redesign of a room photo with shop links. | One retailer, or "similar" items instead of the exact ones shown. No real dimensions. |
| [RoomGPT, Interior AI, DecorAI and about a dozen more](https://decorai.io/blog/best-ai-interior-design-tools.md) | Pretty images in seconds, $9 to $49 a month. | Many quietly change the room's structure. Products are invented. Nothing is checked for fit. |
| [Planner 5D, Homestyler, Coohom](https://www.mogul.com/post/shoppable-interior-design-apps) | Full 3D planners with large model libraries. | Manual work. The user is the designer. Weak link to buying. |
| [Havenly, Decorilla](https://decorai.io/blog/best-ai-interior-design-tools.md) | Human designers, shoppable lists, 3 options per room. | $99 to $349 per room, slow, US-focused. |
| [Houzz, DecorMatters](https://www.mogul.com/post/shoppable-interior-design-apps) | AR preview of a single item at scale. | One piece at a time, not a whole room concept. |
| [Roomstory.ai](https://www.dqindia.com/news/roomstoryai-raises-3-cr-to-redefine-interior-shopping-with-ai-9347671) (India) | Curated designed rooms where every item links to a partner retailer. Raised 3 crore rupees pre-seed in June 2025. | Inspiration rooms, not the user's own measured room. |
| Livspace, HomeLane (India) | Full-service interiors with execution. | High ticket, sales-led. Not self-serve, not for a 50,000 rupee refresh. |

The gap to own: "your actual room, real products that fit, across brands, at your budget, in 2 minutes". Image generators cannot follow because they have no geometry. Retailers cannot follow because they will not show competitors' products.
