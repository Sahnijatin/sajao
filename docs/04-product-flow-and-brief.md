# Product flow and the brief

## Screens

1. **Home.** Saved rooms, start a new room.
2. **Choose room.** Living room or bedroom.
3. **Scan.** Coach intro, then a slow walk around the room with live guides ([room capture](06-room-capture.md)).
4. **Plan drafted.** The plan draws itself with suggested doors, windows and existing items.
5. **Measure.** The app highlights one wall at a time: length, width, then ceiling height. Each value snaps onto the plan with a haptic tick. Extra segments only for non-rectangular rooms.
6. **Confirm.** Drag doors and windows if needed; mark each existing item keep, replace or remove. Verified badge.
7. **Brief.** The form below.
8. **Designs, in stages.** Plan and 3D designs appear first; the photo render of the opened design follows. No spinner anywhere.
9. **Edit.** Drag, rotate, swap, lock, regenerate, day and night, paint and flooring preview.
10. **Buy.** Tap an item, or the whole-room list grouped by retailer.
11. **Share.** Automatic before and after reel.

The three designs are named by intent: Best value (about 80% of budget), Best match (at budget), Bold (a different layout archetype, up to 10% over). If the room cannot fit what she asked for, she sees an honest result instead (see [design engine](08-design-engine.md)).

**Rearrange-only mode** skips the catalog and re-solves the layout with what she already owns.

## The brief

Target: under 90 seconds (to be measured). Four fields are required; the rest sit under "Fine-tune" with smart defaults.

| Field | Input | Required | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mode | Full makeover, refresh, rearrange only | Yes | Decides whether the catalog is used |
| Budget | Slider, plus "strict" or "can stretch 10%" | Yes | Hard constraint |
| Style | Swipe cards drawn from the six [style profiles](14-style-profiles.md) | Yes | More reliable than style words |
| What to include | Lighting, paint and wall finish, flooring and rugs, curtains, seating or bed, tables, storage, wall art, decor, plants | Yes, pre-ticked | Plants, rug, art and lighting on by default |
| Reference | Upload a photo or video | No | Converted to a style target |
| Colours | Loved and banned colours | No | Palette constraint |
| Who lives here | Kids, pets, elderly, work from home, guests | No | Safety, fabric and layout rules |
| Home type | Owned or rented | No | Rented: no drilling, no paint, movable items |
| Room use | Seats, TV, desk, storage need | No | Furniture program |
| Vastu | Off, guidance, strict | No | Scoring or hard rules ([Vastu](12-vastu.md)) |
| Pincode | Auto or typed | No | Only deliverable items |
| Brands | Preferred and blocked | No | Filter |
| Anything else | Free text | No | Read by the AI designer |
