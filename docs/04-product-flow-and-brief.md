# Product flow and brief

Every stage of the journey is one schema object, so the stages cannot drift apart.

| Stage | You receive | Object |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Input | Photos or video of any space, and optional measurements | `Capture` |
| Understanding | Space profile and constraints | `SpaceProfile` |
| Personalisation | Style, budget, preferences | `TasteProfile`, `Brief` |
| Design | Three proposed transformations | `Design` x3 |
| Visualisation | Redesigned room image | `Render` (from `Design`) |
| Spatial view | Layout with dimensions | `Plan` (from `Design`) |
| Commerce | Products and budget | `ShoppingList` (from `Design`) |
| Action | Save, share, shop | `Event` log |

## Screens

On first use, before anything else, one consent screen says in plain words what happens to your photos and that everything can be deleted from Profile, with links to the privacy policy and terms; "Agree and continue" is asked once, and again only when that wording changes or after the account is deleted.

1. **My home.** The app opens here. Before the first room: one before and after, one line and "Add a room". After that: a card per room (hero still, name, last updated, number of designs) and "Add a room". "Add a room" opens Take photos. Beside it, "Use photos or a video" makes a room from pictures already on the phone, through the phone's own picker. Guest session starts silently.
2. **Take photos.** Photos are the main way in; the AR scan is the upgrade. A short note first: step back so the floor shows, put away anything private, keep people out of the picture. Then the camera, landscape only: the shutter works only while the phone is held sideways, and a photo that still comes out upright is not kept. One to three photos. Each is looked at once: "Happy with the view and lighting?" with Retake or Continue, and "Add another view" until there are three. Continue makes the room on My home and starts the upload, then goes on to the Space Profile. A room from photos is at the estimated level: its designs are labelled illustrative with "Measure to confirm", and nothing is claimed to fit until it is measured.
3. **Measure (scan and review).** "Measure this room for exact fit" opens the AR scan, the upgrade that turns an estimated room into a measured one. No room-type picker: scan whatever you want redone, a whole room, one corner or the balcony. The camera fills the screen with one thin progress ring and one plain instruction at a time ("Move slowly around the room", "Get the corners"). Coverage ticks softly under your fingers as it grows, and the moment there is enough the screen says so ("You have enough. Tap Done"): Done arrives inside the ring with a small swell and a tick, and nothing asks for more after that. The scan never asks you to walk around a named object. A nudge shows only when something needs fixing (too dark, too fast, glare); the scan pauses softly, keeps everything so far, and you can keep going anyway. The mesh, guardrail chips, compass map and live labels are diagnostics, shown only with the debug flag in development and preview builds. Done opens the review before anything is uploaded: how much of the room is in, what is still missing in plain words ("The side behind you", "The floor"), and a rough size when the scan saw two pairs of facing walls. "Rescan this part" carries on the same scan and points at the missing part; "Looks good" starts the upload.
4. **Space Profile.** Shown after Continue on Take photos, or "Looks good" on a scan, for every room. "We found a living room": the detected space type and size, with a Change action, drawn over your photo with the error band. The whole of what was captured is redone; there is no "Just a part". Drag a door, mark each existing item keep, replace or remove, optionally add tape values (each snaps in with a haptic tick and re-fits the space).
5. **Taste.** Asked the first time only, between the Space Profile and the stages; later rooms reuse it, and it can be changed from Profile. The first room's budget is asked here; every later room is asked its own budget on one screen, prefilled from the last room, before its designs. Age band and household (two taps), 8 to 12 adaptive swipes, optional references, budget slider with "strict" or "can stretch 10%", must-haves and banned colours, "consider Vastu" toggle. Ends with your Taste Profile in plain words, editable.
6. **Sign in.** Asked only at "Design my room": Truecaller, Google, Apple, WhatsApp OTP fallback.
7. **Generating.** Three stages in plain words, each ticking off as it finishes: "Measuring your walls", "Finding your furniture", "Designing three looks". No bar that just ends: the last tick hands straight on to the reveal. With a server configured the stages are the room's real job (the scan uploads, the room is created, then measured and its contents found), then its design request: the brief is made on the phone from the taste and budget given, and asking again sends the same request; if a stage fails the screen says what happened in plain words and offers the way back to My home, and the scan is never lost. Push when ready.
8. **Reveal.** First your room itself, in your own photos; then three swipeable designs (Best value, Best match, Bold), each with the total cost read from its `Design`. Until the API serves a rendered picture of each look, a look is shown beside your photo, marked "Your room today". A room whose sizes are estimated from photos is marked illustrative, with "measure to confirm before you buy", and makes no fit claims. A room only ever shows what the room and design API sent for it. Until that exists, in every build, the room says plainly that it is not ready yet; a sample is never shown as someone's room. Development and preview builds keep sample content for their preview screens only.
9. **Room.** One screen per room with exactly three tabs. Room: built on hero photos and measured dimensions, not a 3D viewer, since the app may launch without one. It shows the hero views (fixed viewpoints, no free movement), then what was measured, as clean values ("Wall A", "420 cm"): floor area and ceiling height, every wall by letter, doors and windows with the wall each is in, and the big items already in the room (longest side at least 0.8 m, at most eight). Tapping a value shows its source and error band; a value whose band is wider than 5 cm carries a small "approx." beside it. A place is kept for the 3D view behind a flag (`EXPO_PUBLIC_TWIN_VIEWER`), off by default, and the dimensioned 2D plan: the room to scale with the chosen look's furniture in place, each wall's length, the tightest gaps between an item and a wall (measured rooms only), and a soft edge on walls not yet checked with a tape, as wide as their error band. The plan is drawn from the `Design` and the room's floor polygon, whatever its shape: a wall is a solid line with its length, an edge the twin marks open (a doorway, the way into the next area, a stretch not scanned) is a light dashed line and is not listed as a wall, and clearances are measured to walls only. Tapping a piece on the plan shows its product's own width, depth and height and, on a measured room, its gap to the nearest wall; an estimated room says gaps show once it is measured, and its Designs tab says the look is illustrative instead of claiming a fit. Drag and rotate come with the plan editor. Designs: the three looks, "why this works", and a Vastu view that switches on only when the scan has a compass reading. Shopping list: the chosen look's products, each with its own photo, price, buy link and fit status, and the design's total. Fit is the verifier's verdict for that item when the design carries one ("Fits with 12 cm to spare", "A tight fit", "Does not fit here"), and the design's verdict otherwise; an item that does not fit cannot be bought from the list. The look and its list are views of one `Design`. No floor-plan import, no in-app checkout. Above the measurements the room says how its numbers were got, in plain words: checked with a tape, measured by the scan, or estimated. An estimated room offers "Measure this room for exact fit" on a phone that can run the AR scan: the scan measures that same room, which keeps its name and photos and asks for its designs again. The two-tape-value step joins it once the API takes tape values for an existing room. Its shopping list says to measure before it says anything fits. A scan that went through several areas (split by the server at doorway-width gaps) asks once, after it is measured, which area to redesign, with a photo of each; the Room tab then shows every area, and Designs and Shopping list belong to the chosen one. Any space can be scanned, but designs are made for living rooms and bedrooms at launch: a room of any other kind shows its Room tab as usual, and its Designs and Shopping list tabs say plainly that designs for this kind of space are coming. Swap per item comes with the plan editor.
10. **Plan.** Dimensioned 2D plan; drag and rotate re-run the verifier live with haptic snaps.
11. **Shopping list.** Total, breakdown by category, cheaper alternative and rent option per item, grouped by retailer, buy links through our redirect.
12. **Share.** Before and after reel, 9:16, with a link that opens the design in the app, or the landing page for people without it (a web viewer comes after launch).
13. **Vastu.** No tab of its own. Vastu lives as the Vastu view on each room's Designs tab: direction, room scorecard and guidance for that room, on only with a compass reading; deeper help later.
14. **Saved designs and history.** Reached from each room on My home.

## The brief

The `Brief` combines the Taste Profile with per-space choices: budget and flexibility, intent (refresh, full makeover, rearrange only), must-haves, items to keep, Vastu preference. It is the only input the design engine reads besides the Space Profile and the catalog.
