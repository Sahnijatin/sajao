# Vastu

Vastu guidance is offered as close to correct as a phone allows, and labelled honestly: "Vastu guidance, as close as possible. For a full consultation, speak to an expert." The rule set is drafted from published Vastu sources and reviewed by an expert consultant before launch.

## Getting direction right

Phone compasses inside concrete buildings can be off by tens of degrees, so a compass reading alone is never trusted. Three sources are combined:

1. **Compass during the scan.** Headings are logged across the whole sweep and averaged, with a calibration prompt when readings are unstable.
2. **Building outline.** From the phone's location, the building footprint is looked up in open building datasets (Google Open Buildings covers India). Room walls in Indian flats almost always run parallel to the building's outer walls, so the outline gives the exact angle; the compass only has to choose which of the four sides is north.
3. **One-tap confirmation.** A map view: "Your main door faces roughly East?"

If the outline is not found or the sources disagree, the app says direction is approximate and Vastu stays in guidance mode only.

## Room-level Vastu

Applies to the scanned room using its orientation:

- Bed head direction and bed placement in the bedroom.
- Heavy furniture weighted to the south-west.
- TV wall, seating direction and entrance clearance in the living room.
- Desk facing for work-from-home corners.
- Mirror placement, colour guidance by zone, plant and water element placement.

## Whole-home Vastu

The 16-zone analysis needs the whole flat plan:

- **Floor plan photo.** Most flat owners have the builder's brochure plan. A vision model reads rooms, doors and the main entrance; she confirms.
- **Fallback.** She marks where the room sits in the home on a simple 3 by 3 grid.

This gives the zone of each room, the centre of the home, and the main entrance direction.

## Modes

| Mode | Behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| Off | No Vastu influence |
| Guidance | Vastu adds to the soft score; designs show which Vastu points they meet |
| Strict | Key rules become hard constraints for the solver (for example, bed head never toward north) |

## Testing

A set of expert-approved test rooms with known correct answers runs in the evaluation suite on every change. Orientation error is measured on the golden set.
