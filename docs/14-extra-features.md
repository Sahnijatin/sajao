# Extra features worth adding

The best additions use data we already have, the measured room, to answer questions no competitor can.

**For the user**

| Feature | Why she cares | Build cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Delivery path check | Will the sofa get through the door, lift and stairs? A top reason for furniture returns. | Low: 3 extra measurements |
| Paint calculator | Litres, cost and labour estimate from true wall area | Low |
| Staged buying plan | "Month 1 essentials, month 2 comfort, month 3 decor" within her budget | Low |
| Renter mode | No drilling, no paint, everything movable or rentable | Low |
| Family vote link | Partner or parents open the design in a browser and react | Medium, and it brings new users |
| Price-drop and restock alerts | Saves money on a saved design | Low, and it restarts affiliate tracking |
| Before and after share card | Something to post | Low |
| Kid-safe and pet-safe filter | Rounded corners, washable fabric, stable shelves | Low: catalog tags |
| Festive refresh packs | A decor-only update under 5,000 rupees for Diwali or a housewarming | Low |
| Whole-home style memory | The next room matches the first | Medium, later |
| Designer review | A human checks the plan for a fixed fee | Operational |

**For the business**

- **Flat-type library.** One scan per layout in a housing society serves every identical flat. Partner with builders at handover, when buying intent peaks.
- **Brand dashboard.** Show partner brands which sizes, colours and price points users search for and do not find.
- **Pro tier.** Interior designers and brokers use the same engine for clients and staging.
- **Referral credits.** A free photoreal render for each invited friend who scans a room.
- **Web viewer.** Shared designs are indexable pages that bring search traffic.
